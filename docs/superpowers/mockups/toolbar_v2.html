Only the "New Tab" label and the field text differ. The app keeps a serif face for content; for 13 px control labels a sans face sets more evenly, so this is worth a side-by-side in the real window.

Height38 px bar (was 32), 28 px buttons, 26 px inside chips, 6 px between items, 8 px edge padding.
ChipsOne raised background (#2e2a20, 8 px radius, 2 px padding) per related cluster: library actions, zoom, navigation, help and settings.
PrimaryOne solid-gold button per bar. In Tab view the address field takes that weight instead, with an accent ring while focused.
FieldsInset: white at 6% fill, white at 14% hairline, 6 px radius, a leading icon or favicon, and the Ctrl L hint at rest.
Icons16 px, 1.6 px stroke, dim until hovered. "Graph" becomes the node-graph icon and "Tab actions" becomes the three-dot icon, both with tooltips naming their keys.
DividersOne, before the filter, to split the actions from the search. Chips separate everything else by their background, not by lines.
